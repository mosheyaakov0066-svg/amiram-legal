Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Amiram ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Amiram mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account data: If you create an account, we collect your email address and (optionally) your name. You may also use Apple Sign In, in which case we receive only what Apple shares (email and display name).
  • Study preferences: exam date, daily study goal, notification settings, and focus areas — stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your account.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: which screens you visit, which questions you answer, accuracy rates, session lengths. Used to improve the adaptive learning engine and personalise your experience.
  • Device data: device type, operating system version, and language settings. Used to ensure the App displays correctly.
  • Crash and error data: if crash reporting is enabled, anonymised stack traces are sent to our crash reporting provider (Sentry). These do not contain personal information.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or microphone.
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — authentication and account storage (supabase.com/privacy)

  • Sentry — crash reporting (sentry.io/privacy)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (revenuecat.com/privacy)
  • Apple — payment processing and Sign In (apple.com/legal/privacy)

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Study progress stored locally on your device is controlled entirely by you and is deleted when you uninstall the App or reset progress from Settings.

Your Rights

  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to processing or request restriction
  • Data portability
